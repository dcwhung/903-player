### Follow-up：6 個建議 edge test（未收錄，同上次一樣）

完整檔案：QA scratchpad `qa/s551/qaS551Edge.test.ts`（寫法見 [s551 QA §3](2026-10-29_qa_s551-lockscreen-stall.md#3-主動測試補寫-edge-case)）。

1. should stop at exactly STALL_MAX_RETRIES reloads when audio never arrives, and keep reporting stalled
2. should still recover via lock-screen play after the retry budget is spent
3. should resume from the lock-screen seek target when a seek happens while stalled
4. should never overwrite saved progress with 0 across all stalled retries
5. should hit the 30 s cap and then retry even if data keeps trickling in（S-583 之後要用 rs ≤ 2 / 未 `playing` 嘅 premise，否則 4 s 就 retry）
6. should not treat a readyState drop or a buffered gap at the playhead as progress

### iPhone + AirPods checklist

1. 版本號顯示 **v1.20.7**（PWA 自動更新）
2. 前台撳播：1 秒內出聲，鎖屏顯示「播緊」
3. 播緊鎖機，鎖屏 / AirPods 暫停再播：原位繼續，幾秒內出聲
4. 拔 AirPods → 再戴 → 鎖屏撳 Play（S-583）：冇聲嘅話約 4 秒內自己重載出聲，唔係等 8 秒
5. 自動接住播：拉去一集最尾 30 秒鎖機，下一集自己開始有聲
6. 卡住：播緊時 Wi-Fi 轉 4G。冇聲約 4–8 秒內鎖屏轉「Play」再自己接返；或者撳 Play 即刻由原位重來
7. 撳播後 4 秒內亮屏 / 切 app 再返嚟：唔應該斷
8. 卡過之後開返嗰集：續播位置冇被洗走
9. 慢網（如有）：開播後複製 log，留意 `loadstart` 之後第一行 `progress` 喺 `loadedmetadata` 之前定之後、`rs=`，同埋有冇 `stalled` / `retry`（CUI-0011）
